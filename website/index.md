## Why Futon?

**Futon is a community-maintained fork of Kotatsu**, continuing its legacy of providing an excellent manga reading experience on Android. We're committed to maintaining the quality and features that made Kotatsu great while adding community-requested enhancements.

### Key Features

- **Free & Open Source** - GPL-3.0 licensed, fully transparent
- **No Ads or Tracking** - Your reading is private
- **Extensive Source Support** - 1000+ manga sources and growing
- **Active Development** - Community-driven improvements
- **Backup & Sync** - Never lose your library
