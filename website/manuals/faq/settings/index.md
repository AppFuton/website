# Settings

Frequently Asked Questions about various app settings.

## Why is taking screenshots blocked?

Turn off **Screenshot policy** in Reader settings.

## How do I change the app language?

The app follows your system language by default. You can change your Android system language to change the app language.

## How do I enable dark mode?

Go to Settings → Appearance to toggle between light, dark, or system theme.
