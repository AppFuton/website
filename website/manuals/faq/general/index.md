# General

Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Futon?

Futon is an open-source **Android** app that lets you read manga from your device - faster and easier than ever. It's a fork of Kotatsu with community-driven development.

::: tip Disclaimer
Futon is not hosting any content. We are not affiliated with or responsible for any source that is slow, down, missing chapters, or has subpar image quality.
:::

## Where do I post app issues, bugs or feature requests?

To post app issues, bugs, and feature requests, please open a ticket on GitHub by following the link [here](https://github.com/AppFuton/Futon/issues/new/choose)

## Is Futon available for iOS/iPadOS?

There is no iOS or iPadOS version and neither are there plans for one. Porting is difficult due to the separate codebases of iOS and Android apps.

## Can I read light novels?

**Futon** can't read light novels; it's an image parser, not a text parser.(we are thinking about adding light novel compatibility in the future.)

## Can Futon sync across devices?

Yes, the application supports synchronization between devices.

Learn how to set up sync [here](/manuals/guides/synchronization/)

## What do I do if I have to switch to a new phone?

Please follow these steps:

1. Backup your entire favourites and history from your old device and transfer it to your new phone.
2. Restore the backup.

::: tip Note
More information about backup and restore is listed [here](/manuals/guides/backups/).
:::

## How is Futon related to Kotatsu?

**Futon is a fork of Kotatsu.** We maintain our own development while acknowledging and thanking the original Kotatsu developers for their excellent foundation.
